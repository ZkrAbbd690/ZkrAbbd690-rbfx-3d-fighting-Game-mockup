## The Strategy: Projected Decals via `DecalSet`

Urho3D has a built-in `DecalSet` component that handles **projected texture decals** automatically. Here's how it works:

1. **Create a small scar texture** (programmatically generated — a simple slash mark)

The decal projects orthogonally onto the mesh surface and UV-wraps automatically — no manual UV work needed.
